# World Profiles development measurements

These measurements used the internal v3.1.0 development build. The first public release is [Lumina Physics v1.0.0](v1.0.0.md).

- Build an outdoor world from a saved profile or attach seeded rain to a game's existing track and camera. Six editable looks share independent clock, weather, quality, locks and overrides.
- Coastal cliffs, a river valley, dry highlands and custom heightmaps share one terrain surface across visible ground, wheel contacts, water depth and wind-driven vegetation. Godot 4 and Unity starters can read the resolved profile and palette.
- Cloud history, near-canopy lighting and rough-water solar highlights hold up in motion. A clearing squall leaves surface film and deeper puddles drying at different rates as the camera moves from shelter into daylight.

Open **World profiles** from the local showcase with `npm ci` and `npm run preview`. Compare the same moving headland in all six looks, then explore the flight, road and river scenes. [Integration and capture commands](../24-world-profiles.md)

## Measured playback

Apple M6 with 12 GPU cores, macOS 27.2.1, Chromium 159, 1920 × 1080 output. Each showcase case used a new browser context and 300 measured display intervals following warm-up. Startup includes navigation to the ready signal. Driver and operating-system caches could remain warm. The profile camera and clearing tests used High at full resolution.

The playable capture gallery and raw case measurements are generated under `qa/output/world-profiles-1.0/`. The gallery contains six headland comparisons, three gameplay clips and one continuous 13-second clearing-squall capture. Native adapter validation covered source syntax and packaging. The performance measurements cover the Three.js implementation.

The Extra High WebGL 2 playground held 60.1 fps on the first pass while its simulation averaged 2.54 ms against a 2.50 ms CPU budget. Three isolated repeats on the same build measured 2.27 ms, 1.05 ms, 1.72 ms. The table includes the original measurement.
