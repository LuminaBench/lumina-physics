# Salt Crossing

Collect a tide reading on a cold black-sand shore and drive it across an existing causeway. Start the repository preview and open `/examples/salt-crossing/`.

WASD or arrows walk and drive. Press E at the staff to take the reading, then E again to board the cart. F opens the field lens and dragging the scene orbits the camera. Use **Save survey** to return to the same player position and task. Open `?revision=1` for the original reed and earlier sunset.

The independent author received a host-owned level, a saved player, a Rapier bridge, and a selective physics install. The game owns its plant geometry, route, rain gauges, sky, materials, wheel contact selection and save. The repository example imports the installed module exports through `src/lumina-physics.ts`. The archived standalone build and install were verified separately.

The original and revised sites have 519 reed instances with identical IDs and transforms. Moving the observation date changed geometric sunset from 20:07 to 21:25 local solar time. A 9 mm pool under the left wheels left film on the left bridge planks through both dry wheel contacts. At the same instant the far-bank rain gauge read 0.67 and the player's read zero.
